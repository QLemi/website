How to play

  1. 1
    Choose your character

    Each player secretly picks one character from the room's pool.

  2. 2
    Ask a question

    Take turns asking a yes-or-no question, or use Voice chat to ask aloud.

  3. 3
    Cross them off

    Use your private board to eliminate characters that do not fit.

  4. 4
    Make your guess

    A correct guess wins. A wrong guess is marked on your board and passes the turn.
